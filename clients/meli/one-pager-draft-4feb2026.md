### 2. What We're Adding: Axia, Our AI Coach
Closes the gap between workshop knowledge and daily application.

**Q2 Scope (the real thing):**
1. Assessment debrief — understand your profile
2. Session 1 — set your goals and agenda
3. Follow-up coaching sessions (scheduled)
4. On-demand/emergency sessions

**Not in Q2:** Proactive check-ins, calendar integration, heartbeat outreach, reminders. Just the core coaching relationship.

**Two Flavors:**

| Coach | Audience | Content | Mandate |
|-------|----------|---------|---------|
| **ADN MELI Coach** | General employees | Culture, values, Conscious Business tools | Embody the culture in daily work |
| **Leading at MELI Coach** | Managers/leaders | Developing teams, effectiveness, AI, breaking silos | Lead effectively in the MELI way |

Same mechanism, different content. The workshop teaches it, Axia helps them live it.

### 3. How They Connect (why this makes the investment work harder)

**For the individual:**
- Axia helps you advance YOUR agenda — your goals, your challenges
- The workshop gave you the tools, Axia helps you apply them when it matters
- Anchored in what matters to you, not just what the company wants

**For MELI Learning org:**
- Your investment in workshops actually sticks
- Visibility into what people are actually working on (not just attendance)
- Because individual goals align with MELI culture values, individual progress = organizational progress
