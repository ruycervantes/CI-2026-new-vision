# Axia: AI Coach for MELI Leadership Programs

**Closing the gap between workshop knowledge and daily application**

---

## The Opportunity

MELI's leadership workshops are highly effective — 96% favorability, 98% applicability. Leaders leave inspired and equipped with powerful tools.

But workshops are moments, not journeys. Without reinforcement, people return to old patterns. The investment in learning doesn't fully translate to sustained behavior change.

**Axia closes that gap.**

---

## What We're Proposing

Axia is an AI Coach that helps leaders apply what they learned — in the moments that matter.

| What Axia Does | How It Works |
|----------------|--------------|
| **Assessment Debrief** | Understand your personal profile and development areas |
| **Goal-Setting Session** | Define YOUR agenda — what you want to work on |
| **Coaching Sessions** | Regular sessions to work through real challenges using workshop tools |
| **On-Demand Support** | Quick help when you need it — before a tough conversation, after a setback |

---

## Two Flavors, Same Mechanism

| Program | Audience | What Axia Coaches |
|---------|----------|-------------------|
| **ADN MELI** | General employees | Culture, values, and Conscious Business tools — applying MELI's DNA in daily work |
| **Leading at MELI** | Managers & leaders | Building teams, breaking silos, leading with startup mentality, generating value with AI |

The workshop teaches the skills. Axia helps leaders live them.

---

## Value for MELI

**For your leaders:**
- A coach that helps them advance their own agenda
- Support in applying tools to real situations — not theory, practice
- Available when they need it, not just during scheduled workshops

**For MELI Learning & Culture:**
- Your investment in workshops actually sticks
- Visibility into what leaders are working on (not just attendance)
- Reinforcement that scales — every leader gets coaching support
