# MELI Axia Diagrams

Diagrams for Laura's MELI presentation explaining how Axia AI Coach fits into their ecosystem.

---

## 1. The Bridge

**Purpose:** Simple visual metaphor showing Axia bridges the gap between workshop learning and daily application.

**For document section:** Slide 2 (What We're Adding: Axia)

**Prompt:**
```
Create a whiteboard-style diagram showing Axia as a bridge between learning and doing. Hand-drawn, horizontal layout.

TITLE at top: "Bridging the Gap"

LEFT SIDE:
Draw a rounded rectangle containing:
• "WORKSHOP" in bold
• "(Learning)" as subtitle below
• Icon: group of people or classroom
Background: light blue fill

RIGHT SIDE:
Draw a rounded rectangle containing:
• "DAILY WORK" in bold
• "(Doing)" as subtitle below
• Icon: person at desk or briefcase
Background: light blue fill

CENTER - THE BRIDGE:
Draw a bridge shape connecting left to right:
• The bridge is colored orange/gold (#F3B230)
• "AXIA" written on the bridge in bold
• "AI Coach" as small label below the bridge
• The bridge should look like an arc or architectural bridge spanning a gap

THE GAP:
Below the bridge, indicate there's a gap/chasm being bridged. Could be wavy lines suggesting water or just empty space labeled "The Gap"

Style: Hand-drawn whiteboard sketch. Blue for the two endpoints, orange/gold (#F3B230) for the Axia bridge. White background with subtle texture. Professional but informal, like drawn during a strategy meeting. Minimal and clean - no extra details.
```

**Key Elements Checklist:**
- [ ] WORKSHOP box on left (blue)
- [ ] DAILY WORK box on right (blue)
- [ ] AXIA bridge in center (orange #F3B230)
- [ ] Gap indicated below bridge
- [ ] Clean, minimal design

**Output filename:** `meli-1-bridge.png`

## 2. The Three Lanes (Two Paths + Intelligence Layer)

**Purpose:** Shows how Group Path, Individual Path (Axia), and Intelligence Layer work together as an ecosystem.

**For document section:** Slide 3 (Why This Works: How They Connect) or as a standalone ecosystem slide

**Prompt:**
```
Create a whiteboard-style diagram showing three horizontal lanes of a leadership development ecosystem. Hand-drawn, horizontal flow.

TITLE at top: "The MELI Leadership Ecosystem"

TOP LANE - GROUP PATH (blue background stripe):
Label on left: "GROUP PATH" with icon of multiple people
Three boxes flowing left to right with arrows:
• "Workshop" (icon: presentation screen)
• "Offsite" (icon: building or retreat)
• "Facilitated Sessions" (icon: people in circle)
Annotation: "What MELI already does"

MIDDLE LANE - INDIVIDUAL PATH (orange/gold #F3B230 background stripe):
Label on left: "INDIVIDUAL PATH" with icon of single person
Four boxes flowing left to right with arrows:
• "Assessment" (icon: clipboard or checklist)
• "Goals" (icon: target)
• "Coaching Sessions" (icon: chat bubble)
• "On-Demand" (icon: phone or lightning bolt)
Large label: "AXIA - AI Coach" spanning this lane
Annotation: "Personalized reinforcement"

Draw VERTICAL DASHED ARROWS from each element in Group Path down to the Individual Path, showing that Axia supports/reinforces what happens in workshops.

BOTTOM LANE - INTELLIGENCE LAYER (green or gray background stripe):
Label on left: "INTELLIGENCE LAYER" with icon of dashboard or chart
Content spans the width:
• "Visibility for Learning Org"
• "Progress Patterns"
• "What leaders are working on"
Icon: dashboard with graphs
Annotation: "Insights that inform the program"

Draw VERTICAL DASHED ARROWS from Individual Path down to Intelligence Layer, showing data flows down.

At the bottom: "Individual progress = Organizational progress"

Style: Hand-drawn whiteboard sketch. Blue for Group Path, orange/gold (#F3B230) for Individual Path (Axia), green or gray for Intelligence Layer. White background with subtle texture. Professional but informal, like drawn during a strategy meeting. Clear lane structure with visual hierarchy.
```

**Key Elements Checklist:**
- [ ] Three distinct horizontal lanes
- [ ] GROUP PATH: Workshop → Offsite → Facilitated Sessions (blue)
- [ ] INDIVIDUAL PATH: Assessment → Goals → Coaching → On-Demand (orange)
- [ ] INTELLIGENCE LAYER: Visibility, patterns, insights (green/gray)
- [ ] Vertical arrows showing connections between lanes
- [ ] AXIA label prominent on middle lane
- [ ] Annotations explaining each lane

**Output filename:** `meli-2-three-lanes.png`
