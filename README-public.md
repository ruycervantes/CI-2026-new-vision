Shareable knowledge base for the Stoic platform (AI-powered leadership development). Contains platform vision, product specs, roadmap, research, and team effectiveness methodology.

**We help humans have better conversations** — with themselves, with others, as teams, as organizations — through AI that increases consciousness.
