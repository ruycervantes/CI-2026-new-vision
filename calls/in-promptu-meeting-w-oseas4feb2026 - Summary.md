## 6. Discoverability Mechanisms

**Status:** Design requirements outlined

**What was discussed:**
Two mechanisms to help users develop mental model of available capabilities:

**Mechanism 1: Interface discoverability**
- Onboarding explains 3-4 core use cases explicitly
- Interface shows use cases visibly (buttons for coaching session, emergency help, career progression)
- New chat suggestions (like ChatGPT/Claude)

**Mechanism 2: Proactive personalized check-ins**
- Don't send generic check-ins ("how's it going?")
- Send context-aware check-ins based on what you know about them
- Connect to calendar if available
- Example: "Me acordé que estabas un poco nervioso de la reunión con tu jefe... ¿ya sabes algo más?"

**Key insight:**
> "Ya habiendo empezado la primera sesión vas a tener contexto como para hacerle alguna pregunta por ahí de la mitad de la semana."
