## Executive Summary

Preparation call for Laura's upcoming MELI meeting. Ruy and Nelson walked through the ecosystem proposal positioning AI Coach as the thread connecting workshops to day-to-day behavior change. Key takeaway: the AI Coach is not another standalone chatbot—it's a unified coaching experience that uses MELI's culture frameworks, connects to their systems (Google Chat/Slack), and provides organizational visibility into learning progress.

---

## 1. Positioning: Bridge the Workshop Gap

**What was discussed:**
- Frame the offering as what "ensures workshop learning transfers to day-to-day" (vs framing as a "gap" between workshops and reality)
- Laura suggested positive framing: "asegura que se baje" rather than "bridging the gap"

**Key insight:**
> "Esto, como un refuerzo para que realmente eso que aprendiste lo puedas aplicar en tu día a día."

---

## 2. The "One Coach" Concept

**What was discussed:**
- Moving from multiple specialized chatbots (difficult conversations, mindsets, etc.) to **one integrated coaching experience**
- The coach accompanies you across multiple sessions with memory of who you are
- Session flow: Assessment → Workshop → Coaching Kickoff (establish goals) → Multiple sessions → Maintenance

**Key quote (Ruy):**
> "Pasamos de tener que entrar a la lección de conversaciones difíciles... tienes un solo agente, el cual de todo... y dependiendo de lo que tú quieras hacer, toma el conocimiento, la habilidad que tiene y lo aplica."

## 4. Two Program Tracks

### ADN MELI (Individual Contributors / Supervisors)
- Webinar kickoff (can be multiple sessions for different time zones)
- Individual assessment + goal setting
- Stage-gated progression:
  - Stage 1: Complete assessment + goals
  - Stage 2: Access to topic webinars + AI Coach + content library
  - Stage 3: Access to Group Coaching (requires participation thresholds)
- Gamification element: "si no has hecho esto, no puedes pasar a la siguiente etapa"

### Leading at MELI (Leaders)
- Same AI Coach, different "flavor" — generates different reflections
- CB Assessment connects to "4 leadership questions" from workshops
- Presencial workshops non-negotiable (MELI's "ejecutar con excelencia" mindset)

**Key insight (Nelson):**
> "Cada Leading at MELI tiene un coach distinto del de ADN MELI. Porque te va a generar otro de reflexiones."

## 5. Integration Strategy

**What's ready (Q2-Q3 launch):**
- Notifications via Google Chat or Slack ("en 15 minutos es nuestra sesión, pícale aquí")
- Calendar integration for session scheduling
- SSO/Single Sign-On (Google)

**On roadmap (later 2026):**
- Post-meeting follow-ups ("¿cómo te fue en la reunión que dijiste que ibas a practicar?")
- Calendar-aware proactive prompts
- LMS embedding (if needed by MELI)

**MELI context:** They already have "Bernie" — a bot that uses location/calendar to push contextual prompts. They want similar but worry about spam.

**Key quote (Ruy):**
> "Lo súper importante es que el coach esté metido en tu flujo del día a día y no tengas que acordarte dónde está."

## 12. Content Library vs AI Coach

**What was discussed:**

**Differentiation vs ChatGPT:**
