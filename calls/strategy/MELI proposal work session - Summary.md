## Executive Summary

Laura briefed Oseas and Leo on MercadoLibre's request for a "learning ecosystem" to support their two leadership development tracks: **ADN MELI** (culture/values) and **Leading at MELI** (leadership skills). The team brainstormed an integrated solution combining workshops, AI coaches, micro-habits, dashboards, and community elements. Key constraints: MELI doesn't want another platform — any tech must integrate with their existing Google Chat or internal tools (MELI Tools portal). ## 1. MELI's Organizational Context

**What was discussed:**
- Learning sits under Culture & Experience (unusual structure — not under Talent)
- Audience sizes:
  - Senior Managers: 1,400
  - Managers: 2,800
  - Directors/Senior Directors: ~500
  - Shipping Supervisors: 1,500+ (currently unreached)
  - Specialists/Experts: significant number (non-managers)
- Total addressable: ~125,000 employees
- They've done 150 workshops/year at 20-30 people each

**Key insight:** Learning team comes from culture background, so they're focused on beliefs/mindset, not just skills. This is why Axialent fits.

---

## 2. MELI's Two-Track Strategy

**ADN MELI Track:**
- 2-day in-person workshop on culture, leadership principles, mindset
- 96% favorability, 98% applicability ratings
- Recurring: onboarding + every 2 years refresh
- Covers: Conscious Business concepts, mental models (protagonist/learner), communication

**Leading at MELI Track (new):**
- Focus: "How to build teams to win"
- Four pillars:
  1. How do I build teams to win?
  2. How am I effective and break silos through conversations?
  3. How do I lead with startup mentality?
  4. How do I generate value with AI / lead change?
- Format: 4-hour workshops, quarterly or bi-monthly (TBD)

**Decision made:** MELI wants to move from isolated workshops to a sustained "learning ecosystem."

---

## 3. What MELI Wants: Learning Ecosystem

**Three mechanisms they envision:**
1. **In-person workshops** (what they already do well)
2. **Webinars** — context-responsive, expert-led, lighter touch
3. **AI as enabler** — for prework, practice, follow-up, not just delivery

**Key constraint:** NO new platforms. Must integrate with existing:
- Google Chat (official company chat)
- Slack (IT teams only)
- MELI Tools portal (internal app hub)
- Their LMS (unspecified)

**Stated pain:** They feel "anacrónico" (outdated) — still emailing PDFs after workshops. They want 2026-level integration.

---

## 4. Oseas's Proposed Solution Design

Oseas shared a whiteboard with three phases:

### Phase 1: Launch (First 30 days around a workshop)

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Assessment** | CB Assessment customized to MELI language (not generic LSI) |
| **Kick-off/Debrief** | Can be in-person or hybrid; AI debrief option |
| **Workshop** | Existing 2-day ADN MELI or Leading at MELI |
| **Micro-habits** | Post-workshop commitment to specific behavior changes |
| **AI Coach check-ins** | Support for micro-habit practice |
| **Team dashboard** | Learning team gets visibility on engagement, progress |
| **Personal dashboard** | Individual tracking of commitments (new development needed) |
| **Reminders** | Automated nudges for habit reinforcement |

### Phase 2: Continuity

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Community** | With caveat — hard to get traction, but MELI participants crave connection |
| **AI Coaches** | Ongoing access, potentially themed by pillar (startup mindset coach, etc.) |
| **Monthly/bi-monthly sessions** | Sync reinforcement touchpoints |
| **Webinars** | Context-relevant, branded within the program |
| **Peer sessions** | Participants facilitate discussions on pillar topics |
| **Challenges** | Monthly micro-habit challenges with coach check-ins |
| **Recognition** | Tie to MELI Awards, badges, directory recognition |

### Phase 3: Scale/Diffusion

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **100% digital track** | For supervisors and others who can't do 2-day workshops |
| **Mentorship roles** | Workshop graduates mentor digital-track participants |
| **"Service" component** | Formal commitment to help other leaders as part of program |

**Resolution:** Customize CB Assessment to MELI language. This requires development but is doable — already discussed for Sigma. Volume justifies the investment.

## 7. Platform Integration Options

**Option A: Google Chat integration**
- Add AI Coach as a contact in Google Chat
- Similar architecture to Teams integration (already advanced)
- Mike says once Teams is done, Google is easier

**Option B: MELI Tools portal**
- They have MELI GPT, NotebookLLM, "Verdi" (agent for nudges)
- Could add a button/link to Stoic Coach
- Potentially easier than full integration — just SSO/backlink

**Option C: Transfer code to their platform**
- Give them the coach configuration
- Charge for access, updates, support
- More trust-based, but feasible with MELI

**Option D: LMS integration**
- Embed in their LMS (useful for supervisors without Google accounts)

**IT approval:** Will be required regardless. Stoic has passed security reviews for Credicorp, Aleatica, Telus, P&G.

## 8. Pricing Model

**Current:** ADN MELI 2-day workshop = $8,200 USD (1 facilitator) for ~35 people = $234/person

**Proposed:** Workshop + ecosystem = ~$9,000-10,000 (10-15% uplift)
- Bundled pricing, not itemized
- Digital components factored into Axialent target
- Internal accounting between Axialent and Stoic handled separately
