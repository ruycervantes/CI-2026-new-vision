## The Facilitation Arc

### 1. Understand Context

- **Culture & Leadership Mindsets** â€” Psychological safety, leadership alignment, fear and resistance, readiness for human-AI collaboration

### 4. Identify High-Impact Opportunities

Given the north star and the current reality, what would actually move things? The facilitator helps the leader surface and prioritize opportunitiesâ€”starting with cultural leverage where it's needed.

### 5. Shape the Plan

What is the leader going to focus on over the next 12 months? What outcomes would indicate progress? What mindsets, skills, or systems need to shift? Who owns what?

The chatbot helps the leader think through these questions without overwhelming themâ€”keeping the conversation grounded in what's realistic and meaningful.

This is where the plan becomes real. Not "here's what I aspire to" but "here's what I'm actually going to do."

- What's the first thing you're going to do?
- When?
- With whom?

A leader might leave with "I'm going to have a conversation with my leadership team next Thursday about our actual fears around AI" or "I'm going to spend two hours this week using the tool myself before asking my team to adopt it."

The commitment is personal and specific.
