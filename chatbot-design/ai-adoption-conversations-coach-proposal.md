# AI Adoption Conversations Coach

## Overview

Rather than prescribing governance frameworks or adoption playbooks, it guides leaders to discover which human conversations are missingâ€”and helps them design those conversations effectively.

## Two Modes

### Troubleshooting Mode

The leader arrives with an AI initiative that isn't working as expectedâ€”adoption has stalled, quality is suffering, resistance is emerging, or a promising pilot isn't scaling. ### Planning Mode

## Ten Conversation Domains

The coach helps leaders explore which of these conversations are needed for their specific situation:

### Foundational

- **Alignment** â€” Why are we doing this? What does success look like? Are stakeholders agreed on the purpose and goals?

- **Inclusion** â€” Who has a voice in shaping this? Whose perspectives are being heard? Who's missing from the table?

### Rules of the Game

- **Roles & Decision Rights** â€” Who decides what? Who has authority to approve, adjust, or stop? Who is accountable for outcomes?

- **Standards** â€” What does "good" look like? What quality bar applies? How do we know if work is acceptable?

- **Expectations** â€” What are people supposed to do? When should they use AI and when shouldn't they? What's required of them?

### Navigating Tensions

- **Risk** â€” What could go wrong? What are we worried about? How do we handle unintended consequences?

- **Trust** â€” Do people feel safe? Do they trust the technology, the process, each other, leadership? What builds or erodes confidence?

- **Ethics** â€” What should we do, not just what can we do? Where are the moral boundaries? What values guide our choices?

### Enabling Conditions

- **Learning & Capability** â€” What do people need to learn? How do we build organizational capacity to work effectively with AI?

- **Communication & Transparency** â€” How does information flow? What gets shared, with whom, when? Who's in the loop?

## The Output

A leader leaves the conversation with clarity on:

- Which conversation they need to have next
- With whom
- About what specific issue
- How to approach it effectively

---

*The goal is not to push a particular solution, but to help leaders understand what conversations they need to have to either set their AI initiative up for success or address the problems it's experiencing.*
