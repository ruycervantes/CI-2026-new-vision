📚 The Geek Way Pillars and Analyzed Behaviors

Understanding what each behavioral signal represents in the Geek Way framework

🔬 Science Pillar

S1 Uses data and metrics to guide decisions
S2 Questions assumptions and validates with evidence
S3 Experiments before committing resources
S4 Learns from failures without blame
S5 Welcomes fact-based dissent
S6 Tracks operational metrics and guardrails

🎯 Ownership Pillar

O1 Single-threaded ownership / DRI
O2 Makes decisions without unnecessary approvals
O3 Focuses on outcomes over activities
O4 Proactively removes blockers with options
O5 Maintains clear contracts (OKRs/SLAs)
O6 Works in small autonomous teams

⚡ Speed Pillar

SP1 Uses short iteration loops and timeboxes
SP2 Makes reversible decisions at ~70% info
SP3 Ships to learn with small batches
SP4 Removes queues and handoffs
SP5 Follows OODA rhythm (observe→orient→decide→act)

🌐 Openness Pillar

OP1 Makes artifacts public by default
OP2 Explicitly invites critique
OP3 Runs pre-mortems to anticipate failures
OP4 Shares failures and learnings visibly
OP5 Broadcasts context and strategy broadly

📊 Understanding Metrics

Coverage = Percentage of users showing ANY evidence of the behavior (reach of the program)
Depth (Maturity Rate) = Percentage of engaged users at Moderate or Strong levels (quality of behavior change)
Why it matters: High coverage with low depth suggests awareness without behavior change. High depth indicates meaningful transformation.
